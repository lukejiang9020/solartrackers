# Solar Tracking Project

## Introduction

Constructed and planned over the summer of 2026, this solar tracker distinguishes itself from the plethora of other solar tracker designs found on the internet. What started out as a replica of a pre-existing solar tracker design has morphed into one that is far more efficient, practical, and unique compared to ones in many other projects.

## Usage

Head over to [Solar Tracking Project](https://lukejiang9020.github.io/solartrackers) to see the process of designing the solar tracker, and results from experiments conducted on energy absorption from sunrise to sunset.
